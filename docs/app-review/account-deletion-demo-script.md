# Account Deletion Review Recording

Use a clean macOS test profile and the review test account. Record the complete window without cutting away from the interaction.

1. Launch AiOffice and show the home screen.
2. Click the account button in the lower-left corner.
3. Select **Account** and show the in-app email/Apple/Lightyu sign-in choices.
4. Sign in with the review test account.
5. Reopen the account page and show the signed-in account profile.
6. Click **Delete account**.
7. Show the warning that the action cannot be undone and that local documents remain on the Mac.
8. Enter `删除账号` in the confirmation field.
9. Click **Permanently delete**.
10. Show that the account page closes and the lower-left account control returns to the signed-out state.
11. Reopen the account page to show that the in-app sign-in page is available again.

The recording should not show an email verification code, access token, API key, payment card, or private document content.
